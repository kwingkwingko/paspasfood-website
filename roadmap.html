Product Roadmap

Our journey from concept to Sindangan's leading delivery super-app — and beyond.

● In Progress
Q1 2026 — Present

Development Phase

Building the complete PaspasFood platform from the ground up — a full-featured multi-role delivery app.

  • Multi-role system (Customer, Store, Rider, Admin)
  • Food ordering with menus, options & variants
  • Item delivery service (documents, electronics, medical, etc.)
  • Real-time order tracking with live rider map
  • Store management dashboard with products & categories
  • Rider job system with in-app navigation
  • Admin panel with fee configuration & tenant approvals
  • Push notifications with branded alert sounds
  • Coupon & promotions system
  • Ratings, reviews & favorites
  • Order cancel flow with role-based reasons
  • Order merge & edit capabilities
◎ Next Up
Q2 — Q3 2026

Survey & Beta Testing

Conduct market surveys in Sindangan and launch a controlled beta test with local stores, riders, and customers.

  • Community survey — gauge interest from stores, riders & customers
  • Onboard 10–20 pilot store partners in Sindangan
  • Recruit and onboard initial rider fleet
  • Beta testing with real users — feedback collection
  • Bug fixes, UX improvements based on feedback
  • Performance testing & optimization
  • Cash on Delivery (COD) as the initial payment method
  • Finalize delivery fee structure for Sindangan
◎ Planned
Q4 2026

Official Launch in Sindangan

Full public launch of PaspasFood in Sindangan, Zamboanga Del Norte with App Store & Play Store availability.

  • App Store & Google Play Store submission
  • Official launch event & local marketing
  • Onboard 30+ store partners
  • Full rider fleet operations
  • Customer acquisition campaigns
  • Crash analytics & performance monitoring
  • Customer support system
◇ Future
Q1 — Q2 2027

ePayment Integration & Growth Features

Integrate digital payment methods and introduce features to boost user retention and engagement.

  • GCash payment integration (live transactions)
  • Credit/Debit card payment processing
  • Other ePayment options (Maya, etc.)
  • In-app chat between customer, store & rider
  • Loyalty rewards & points system
  • Scheduled / advance orders
  • Re-order from past orders (one-tap)
  • Flash deals & time-limited promotions
  • Referral program for customers & stores
  • Rider wallet & automated payouts
◇ Future
Q3 — Q4 2027

Regional Expansion

Expand PaspasFood to neighboring municipalities in Zamboanga Del Norte and introduce new service verticals.

  • Expand to nearby municipalities (Liloy, Salug, Leon Postigo)
  • AI-powered delivery route optimization
  • Smart order batching for riders
  • Grocery delivery vertical
  • Store analytics dashboard (sales trends, peak hours)
  • Admin analytics (GMV, growth, retention metrics)
  • Multi-language support (Filipino, Bisaya, English)
◆ Vision 2028+
2028 and Beyond

Super-App & Mindanao Expansion

Transform PaspasFood into a full super-app and scale across Mindanao's major cities.

  • Expand to Zamboanga City, Dipolog, Dapitan
  • PaspasFood Wallet — integrated e-wallet
  • Pharmacy & medicine delivery
  • PaspasMart — convenience store on-demand
  • Bill payments & mobile load integration
  • PaspasFood for Business (corporate accounts)
  • Dark kitchen / cloud kitchen partnerships
  • Subscription plans for frequent users
  • Franchise model for rapid regional expansion
Where We Are Now

Currently in Active Development

We are building out the core PaspasFood platform with 50+ features across all four user roles. Our next milestone is conducting community surveys and launching beta testing in Sindangan, Zamboanga Del Norte.
