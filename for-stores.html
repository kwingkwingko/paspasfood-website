Selling on PaspasFood

From signing up to your first order. The two parts people get stuck on are your location pin and how you get paid — both are below.

Step by step

Setting Up Your Store

1

Sign up as a store

Install the app, create an account and choose Store. Verify your email when the code arrives — check your spam folder if it does not.

2

Wait for approval

An admin reviews your account before you can trade. Being approved as a store owner and having a store are two different things — the next step is yours.

3

Create your store

Business name, address, opening hours and a delivery radius. Then add your products and turn the store on.

Do not skip this

Your Location Pin

A store without one exists, but no customer will ever find it.

PaspasFood shows customers the stores near them, and “near” is measured from your coordinates — not from your written address. If your store has no pin it is skipped by every nearby search, and the delivery fee cannot be calculated either.

Getting it right

  • Type your address and pick the suggestion that matches — that is what sets the pin
  • If your shop is not on the map, choose the nearest landmark you know
  • Check the pin before saving: it decides what customers pay for delivery

Delivery radius: how far you are willing to send orders, in kilometres. Sindangan is only a few kilometres across, so 5–10 km covers the town comfortably. The maximum allowed is 100 km, and anything above that is refused.

Important

How You Get Paid

In cash, at the counter, before the food leaves.

1

The rider pays you

When the rider collects the order they pay you for it in cash, out of their own pocket. You are paid before the food leaves your shop.

2

No commission

PaspasFood takes nothing from your sale. You keep the full price of what you sold.

3

Nothing to withdraw

There is no payout to wait for, because the money is already in your till.

Why your Wallet shows ₱0.00

Because you were already paid in cash at pickup. The Wallet is a record of what you sold for your own bookkeeping, not money we are holding. A zero balance there is correct, not a missing payment.

Day to day

Running Your Store

When an order arrives

  1. Accept it, or decline if you cannot make it
  2. Cook it and mark it ready
  3. A rider collects and pays you
  4. Mark it handed over

Staying visible

  • Keep your opening hours accurate — customers only see you when open
  • Mark sold-out items unavailable rather than declining orders
  • Use Temporarily Closed for a holiday instead of deleting products

Approved is not the same as open. A store can be approved, stocked and still invisible because it is switched off or outside its opening hours. If customers cannot find you, check those two first.
