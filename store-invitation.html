The questions shop owners actually ask

So how do you make money?

From the riders, not from you. A rider buys a small credit to accept each job. Your sale is yours.

When exactly do I get paid?

At pickup. The rider hands you cash for the food before they leave with it. The customer pays the rider at their door — that is between the two of them.

What if I am too busy to take orders?

Mark your store closed in the app, in one tap. Orders stop. Open again when you are ready.

Do I need a computer or a printer?

No. A phone is enough. A small Bluetooth receipt printer is handy once you are busy, but it is optional.

Who handles the delivery?

Our riders. You prepare the order and hand it over — the trip, the customer and the cash collection are ours to manage.

What does it cost me if an order is cancelled?

Nothing, if it has not been made yet. You can decline an order you cannot fulfil.
