Store Guide

How you get paid, what you print, and what we expect.
Please read this before your onboarding meeting, and bring your questions.

Partnering with PaspasFood is straightforward: customers order through the app, and a rider collects the order from you. The rider pays you in cash at pickup — not PaspasFood, and not later. There is no joining fee and, at present, no commission on your sales.

1. The basics

Where we operateSindangan and nearby barangays.
How customers payCash on delivery only. No GCash, no cards, no online payment.
What it costs youNo upfront cost to join. No commission on your sales at present.
What you needA smartphone or tablet with the app. Android: install from the Play Store. iPhone: open paspasfood.com in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen — it works just like an app. A Bluetooth receipt printer is optional but recommended.

2. Applying

  1. Download the app and register as a Store Owner.
  2. Submit your business details for review.
  3. Complete the email verification step. Your application is not finished until you do.
  4. We review and approve it, then help you load your menu.

3. How you get paid

The rider pays you in cash, at pickup

You are paid when the rider collects the order — not afterwards, not by transfer, and not by PaspasFood. The amount is the food subtotal, printed in large type on the Store Copy under the words RIDER PAYS.

1. The order arrives in your app, with a sound alert.
2. You accept and prepare it. Print the Order Slip for your kitchen or counter.
3. The rider arrives and pays you the food subtotal, in cash. Print the Store Copy and settle against it.
4. The rider collects the customer's total at the door — that is a bigger number, and the difference is theirs.

Why the customer pays more than you receive

The customer's total includes the delivery fee, any weather surcharge and any tip. Those belong to the rider, who is repaying themselves for the cash they just handed you and earning their wage for the trip. Asking a rider for the customer's total means asking them to hand over their own pay.

Subtotal PHP 497.00 Delivery fee PHP 62.00 Weather surcharge PHP 15.00 Rider tip PHP 10.00 ================================================ Customer pays the rider PHP 584.00 RIDER PAYS YOU PHP 497.00 <-- yours

Your order screen in the app shows both figures: Customer pays rider, and below it Rider pays you. The second is the one you collect.

4. The three documents you can print

Order SlipWhat to prepare — items, quantities, add-ons and notes, with no prices. For your kitchen or counter staff.
Store CopyWhat the rider owes you. Food only. This is the one to settle against.
Customer CopyThe customer's receipt, showing everything they paid. Put it in the bag.

Printing works with a Bluetooth thermal printer on Android — pair it once in Store Settings and afterwards it is one tap. On iPhone and the web app, printing opens your device's normal print dialogue instead.

5. Rules for stores

  • Keep your menu, prices and availability accurate. A customer charged for something out of stock loses trust in all of us.
  • Accept or decline an order promptly. A rider and a customer are both waiting on it.
  • Mark your store closed when you are not trading, rather than letting orders arrive unanswered.
  • Prepare orders to the same standard as a walk-in customer, and pack them so they survive the trip.
  • Hand the rider exactly what is on the slip. If something is unavailable, say so before they pay.
  • Observe food safety and hygiene requirements, and keep your permits current.
  • Do not ask a rider for more than the Store Copy says.
  • Do not take payment outside the platform for an order placed on it.

6. Cancellations

If you cannot fulfil an order, cancel it in the app as early as you can and give the reason. Cancelling reverses the earning for that order.

If an order sits with no rider for a long time, we will prompt you. You decide whether to cancel it or contact the customer — we will not cancel your order automatically and leave you with food nobody asked you to stop making.

7. Losing your partnership

A store account may be suspended or removed for:

  • Dishonesty about an order, an item or a payment.
  • Demanding more money from a rider than the Store Copy states.
  • Persistently listing items that are not available.
  • Food safety or hygiene failures.
  • Abusive or discriminatory behaviour towards riders, customers or staff.
  • Taking payment outside the platform for an order placed on it.

If something goes wrong, contact support in the app — Profile → Help Center — or message our Facebook page. Include the order number; it begins with a #.

8. Before your meeting

Please bring your business permit, a valid ID, and your menu with current prices.

Photographs of your best-selling items help enormously — customers order far more from a menu they can see.

Write down anything here you disagree with or do not understand. The meeting is the right place to settle it.

One last word

These rules exist because real money moves between real people with no bank in the middle. A rider fronts their own cash to pay you; you hand over food before being paid by a customer you never meet. That only works while everyone is straight with each other, and we would rather be clear about it now than apologetic about it later.
