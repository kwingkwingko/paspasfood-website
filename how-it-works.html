How It Works

A step-by-step guide for every user role in the PaspasFood ecosystem.

For Customers

Ordering Food Made Simple

From craving to doorstep in just a few taps.

1

Browse Stores

Open the app, explore nearby stores, browse categories, and view menus with photos and prices.

2

Add to Cart

Select your items, customize with options (size, add-ons), set quantity, and add to cart.

3

Checkout & Pay

Choose your delivery address or pin it on a map, select payment method (COD), apply coupons, and place your order.

4

Track & Receive

Watch your order status update in real-time. Track your rider on the map and receive your food.

For Store Owners

Manage Your Store Effortlessly

From setup to daily orders — everything in your pocket.

1

Register & Setup

Sign up, set up your store profile with logo, address, operating hours, and pin your location on the map.

2

Add Products

Create your menu with categories, products, prices, images, and customizable options (sizes, add-ons).

3

Receive Orders

Get notified instantly when orders come in. Accept, prepare, and mark orders as ready for delivery or pickup.

4

Track & Grow

Monitor rider deliveries, view daily reports with delivery fees and surcharge breakdowns, and track your revenue growth with detailed analytics.

For Riders

Start Earning in Minutes

Accept jobs, navigate, deliver, and get paid.

1

Sign Up

Register as a rider, upload your driver's license and selfie for verification, then wait for admin approval.

2

Browse Jobs

View available food delivery and item delivery jobs. See your net earnings upfront and tap to view full details before accepting.

3

Pick Up & Deliver

Navigate to the store or pickup point using in-app maps, track your earnings in real-time including any weather surcharges, then deliver to the customer.

4

View History

View your completed deliveries with detailed earnings breakdown — delivery fees, weather surcharges, commission, VAT, and net pay.

Item Delivery

Send Items Across Town

A simple process to get your items delivered safely.

1

Set Locations

Enter pickup and drop-off addresses or use "Pin on Map" to drop a pin, add contact details for the recipient.

2

Item Details

Choose item type (document, electronics, medical, etc.), describe the item, add photos, and select size & weight.

3

Submit & Track

Choose vehicle type, review the delivery fee, submit your request, and track the rider in real-time.

For Admins

Full Control at Your Fingertips

A powerful admin panel to manage the entire PaspasFood platform.

1

Dashboard

Get an overview of stores, riders, customers, orders, and deliveries — all at a glance.

2

Manage Approvals

Review, approve, or reject store and rider applications. Filter by status and manage tenant accounts.

3

Monitor Orders

View all platform orders in real-time, filter by status, and cancel problematic orders when needed.

4

Reports & Analytics

View daily, monthly, and yearly reports — revenue, fees, VAT breakdown by source, order volume, and user registration stats.
