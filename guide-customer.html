Customer Guide

Ordering, paying, tracking, and what to do when something goes wrong.

PaspasFood brings food and errands to your door in Sindangan. You order in the app, a local store prepares it, and a rider brings it to you. You pay the rider in cash when it arrives.

1. The basics

Where we deliverSindangan and nearby barangays. If your address is outside our range, the app will tell you when you set it.
How you payCash on delivery only. GCash, cards and other online payments are planned, but not available yet.
Getting the appFree. Android from the Play Store; on iPhone, open paspasfood-app.vercel.app in Safari and choose “Add to Home Screen”.
Your phone numberYou can browse and create an account freely. We send a one-time SMS code the first time you order, so a real person is on the other end before a rider is sent out.

2. Ordering

1. Set your address. Type it, use your current location, or drop a pin on the map.
2. Pick a store and add items. Closed stores are greyed out.
3. Check the total. The delivery fee and the full amount are shown before you confirm — nothing is added afterwards.
4. Place the order. The store confirms it and starts preparing.
5. A rider collects it and brings it to you. Pay them in cash at the door.

What makes up your total

The foodWhat the store charges for the items you chose.
Delivery feeBased on the distance to your address, so it differs per order. Always shown before you confirm.
Weather surchargeAdded only in bad weather. It goes entirely to the rider.
TipOptional, and entirely the rider's.

3. Tracking your order

  • Your order screen shows each stage — confirmed, preparing, almost ready, packing, picked up, on the way.
  • You get an arrival time as soon as the store accepts, worked out from the time the store says it needs plus the ride to you. If the store falls behind and updates that time, your screen updates with it.
  • A rider is found while your order is still being prepared, so they can set off early. You will see when one has been assigned.
  • Once the rider sets off you can watch them approach on a live map, with the arrival time measured from where they actually are.
  • You can message the rider in the app. If your address is hard to find, send a landmark — it genuinely gets your food to you faster.
  • You can also message the store about the order itself.

4. Pasugo — errands

Pasugo is our pabili service. Tell us the shop and what you need, set your spending limit, and a rider buys it with their own money and brings it to you — groceries, medicine, market goods, anything you cannot leave the house for.

  • You pay for the goods plus the delivery fee and any Pasugo fees when it arrives. Every fee is shown before you confirm, and all of it goes to your rider.
  • A fee worked out from your spending limit is the most it can be. If your rider spends less than the limit, it comes down with it.
  • The rider never spends more than the limit you set. The maximum is ₱3,000.
  • Be specific about brands and sizes — the rider is standing in a shop reading what you wrote.

5. Cancelling, and when something is wrong

  • Cancelling: you can cancel from the app while the store has not started preparing. Once preparation has begun, cancelling may not be possible — your order has already been made.
  • Wrong or missing items: message us with your order number, a photo of what arrived, and what was wrong.
  • Order not arriving: check the live map first, then message the rider. If you cannot reach them, contact support.
  • Your receipt: every order keeps a full receipt in the app, showing every amount you paid.

Your order number begins with a # and is at the top of the order screen. Including it gets you a much faster answer.

Please have your cash ready

Your rider has already paid the store for your food out of their own pocket and is repaid when you pay them. They are not carrying a float from a company — it is their own money until you hand yours over. Having the amount ready, and being reachable on your phone, makes a real difference to them.

6. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete my account, or request it at paspasfood.com/delete-account.

7. Getting help

In the app: Profile → Help Center — browse answers or raise a support ticket. You can also message our Facebook page. For anything about an existing order, include the order number.
