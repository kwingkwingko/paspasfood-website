Platform Overview

A comprehensive look at the PaspasFood ecosystem — four roles, one powerful platform.

Our Mission

Empowering Local Commerce Through Technology

PaspasFood bridges the gap between local businesses and customers by providing a seamless, affordable, and real-time delivery platform built for communities in Zamboanga Del Norte.

The Problem We Solve

In municipalities like Sindangan, local food stores and businesses lack the technology to reach customers beyond walk-in traffic. Customers have limited options for convenient food delivery, and riders lack a reliable platform to earn income.

National delivery apps don't serve our area. PaspasFood fills this gap by providing a locally-focused super-app that's built for our community's unique needs.

🇵🇭

Made for Sindangan

Designed from the ground up for the local market in Zamboanga Del Norte, with plans to expand regionally.

The Ecosystem

Four Roles, One Platform

Every user has a tailored experience designed for their specific needs.

🛒 Customer

Order Anything, Anytime

Browse local stores, customize your food order with options and variants, apply coupons, and track your delivery live on the map.

  • ✓ Browse stores, menus & product options
  • ✓ Cart, checkout & coupon system
  • ✓ Real-time order & rider tracking
  • ✓ Item delivery for documents, electronics & more
  • ✓ Ratings, reviews & saved favorites
🏪 Store Owner

Manage & Grow Your Business

Full store management — products, categories, orders, coupons, and daily reports. Accept orders in real-time and track your riders.

  • ✓ Product & category management with variants
  • ✓ Real-time order management & alerts
  • ✓ Track riders & customers on map
  • ✓ Coupon creation & daily reports
🏍️ Rider

Earn on Your Own Schedule

Accept food and item delivery jobs, navigate with built-in maps, upload proof of delivery, and track your earnings.

  • ✓ Browse & accept available jobs
  • ✓ In-app map navigation with routing
  • ✓ Real-time location sharing
  • ✓ Proof of delivery & job history
⚙️ Admin

Full Platform Control

Approve tenants, manage fees, create platform-wide promotions, oversee all orders, and configure the entire system.

  • ✓ Tenant approval workflows
  • ✓ Dynamic fee & VAT configuration
  • ✓ Platform-wide coupon system
  • ✓ Order oversight & user management
