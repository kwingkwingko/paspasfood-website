Rider, Store and Customer Guide

How the platform works, what you earn, and the rules that apply.
Please read this before your onboarding meeting and bring your questions.

PaspasFood connects customers with local stores and riders in Sindangan. We are the platform that puts you in touch with each other — we are not the seller of the food, and we do not handle your money. This guide explains exactly who pays whom, so there are no surprises on your first day.

1. The basics, for everyone

Where we operateSindangan and nearby barangays. If an address falls outside our range, the app will say so when it is entered.
How customers payCash on Delivery only. There is no GCash, card or online payment at this time.
What we deliverFood from partner stores, and Pasugo — errands where a rider buys something for you, up to ₱3,000.
Getting the appFree. Android from the Play Store; on iPhone, open paspasfood.com in Safari and choose “Add to Home Screen”.
Verifying your numberYou can browse and create an account freely. We send a one-time SMS code the first time you place an order or take a job, so we know a real person is on the other end.

2. For Riders

This is the longest section because it is the one where your own money is involved. Please read all of it. If anything here is unclear, ask at the meeting before you start — not after your first job.

2.1 Applying

  1. Download the app and register as a Rider.
  2. Upload a photo of your driver's licence and a selfie holding it.
  3. Complete the email verification step. Your application is not finished until you do.
  4. Our team reviews it. You will be notified in the app when you are approved.

Bicycles, motorcycles and cars are all accepted. You will need a smartphone with mobile data.

2.2 PaspasPoints — how we charge you

We do not take a commission from your earnings. Instead you buy PaspasPoints, and one point is used each time you accept a job.

Price₱8.00 per point
Cost per jobOne point, whatever the order is worth. A ₱1,500 order costs the same one point as a ₱80 one.
How to buyRequest the number you want in the app, then bring the money to the PaspasFood office. Requesting is not buying — points are credited only once we have actually received payment.
Maximum held40 points at a time. This limit exists to cap what you commit to us in advance.
ExpiryPoints never expire.
RefundsPoints are not refundable, not transferable, and have no cash value. They cannot be sold or given to another rider. Buy what you expect to use.

A rider with no points cannot accept work. Keep a few in reserve.

2.3 How the money moves

Read this twice. It is the part riders most often get wrong on day one.

1. You accept the job. One PaspasPoint is held.
2. At the store, you pay for the food — from your own money. The amount is on the printed Store Copy and it is the food only.
3. At the customer's door, you collect the full total — food, delivery fee, any weather surcharge, and any tip.
4. You keep the difference. The delivery fee, the surcharge and the tip are yours. PaspasFood takes no share of any of it.
The store copy and the customer's receipt are different documents

The slip the store prints for you says RIDER PAYS and shows the food subtotal only. The customer's total is a bigger number because it includes the delivery fee, the surcharge and the tip — which are your earnings, not the store's. Never pay a store the customer's total. If a store asks for it, show them this page.

2.4 How much cash you need to start

Because you pay the store first and are repaid at the door, you need money in your pocket before you earn any. On current averages an order is around ₱200, and you may hold two jobs at once, so plan for roughly ₱400 of working cash per shift, plus your points. A large order needs more.

This is a real requirement and we would rather you knew it now than found out at a counter.

Verify before you pay — the most important rule in this guide

Once you have paid the store, that money is yours at risk. If the customer cannot be reached, refuses the order, or the address does not exist, you are left holding the goods and you are out the money you paid. PaspasFood does not reimburse it and does not charge the customer for it.

So before you hand over any cash, check the order:

  • Is the order unusually large or expensive?
  • Is the address unfamiliar, vague or incomplete?
  • Did the customer answer when you called or messaged?

If anything feels wrong, call the customer before you pay. If you still cannot reach them, contact PaspasFood support rather than paying and hoping.

2.5 If a job is cancelled

What happenedYour point

The store cancelsReturned
PaspasFood cancels, or the order cannot be fulfilledReturned
The customer refuses the deliveryReturned
You cancel a job you had already accepted, without a valid reasonUsed

A point is used when you cancel because, while you held that job, nobody else could take it and the customer waited. If you genuinely cannot complete a job — a breakdown, an accident, an emergency — tell support. We are reasonable about real problems.

Note that a returned point does not return money you already paid a store. That is what section 2.4 is about.

2.6 Rules for riders

  • Hold no more than two active jobs at a time. The app enforces this.
  • A photo is required as proof of delivery. You cannot complete a job without one.
  • Check the order against the receipt before you pay the store. Once you have paid, a wrong or missing item is yours to resolve with them.
  • Do not open, consume, substitute or tamper with an order.
  • Do not ask a customer for more than the total shown in the app, for any reason.
  • Keep your phone reachable while you hold a job. Customers are told they can contact you.
  • Treat customers and store staff courteously, whatever the situation.
  • Obey traffic law. Wear a helmet. No delivery is worth an injury.
  • Your account is yours. Do not let anyone else ride on it.

3. For Stores

3.1 Applying

  1. Download the app and register as a Store Owner.
  2. Submit your business details for review.
  3. Complete the email verification step — your application is not finished until you do.
  4. Our team reviews and approves it, then helps you load your menu.

There is no upfront cost to join, and at present we take no commission on your sales.

3.2 How you get paid

The rider pays you in cash, at pickup

You are paid when the rider collects the order — not later, not by transfer, and not by PaspasFood. The amount is the food subtotal, and it is printed in large type on the Store Copy under the words RIDER PAYS.

The customer pays a larger total at their door, because it also includes the delivery fee, any weather surcharge and any tip. Those belong to the rider. Asking a rider for the customer's total means asking them to hand over their own wages.

In the app, your order screen shows both figures clearly: Customer pays rider, and below it Rider pays you — the second is the one you collect.

3.3 The three documents you can print

Order SlipWhat to prepare — items, quantities, add-ons and notes, with no prices. For your kitchen or counter staff.
Store CopyWhat the rider owes you. Food only. This is the one to settle against.
Customer CopyThe customer's receipt, showing everything they paid. Put it in the bag.

Printing works with a Bluetooth thermal printer on Android. On iPhone and the web app, printing opens your device's normal print dialogue instead.

3.4 Rules for stores

  • Keep your menu, prices and availability accurate. A customer who is charged for something out of stock loses trust in all of us.
  • Accept or decline an order promptly. A rider and a customer are both waiting on it.
  • Mark your store closed when you are not trading, rather than letting orders arrive unanswered.
  • Prepare orders to the same standard as a walk-in customer, and pack them so they survive the trip.
  • Hand the rider exactly what is on the slip. If something is unavailable, say so before they pay.
  • Observe food safety and hygiene requirements, and keep your permits current.
  • Do not ask a rider for more than the Store Copy says.

4. For Customers

  • Order through the app, pay the rider cash on delivery, and keep the receipt in the bag.
  • You will see the delivery fee and the full total before you confirm. Nothing is added afterwards.
  • Track your rider on a live map, and message them in the app if your address is hard to find.
  • You can cancel while the store has not started preparing. Once cooking has begun, cancelling may not be possible.
  • Pasugo errands are capped at ₱3,000. The rider buys with their own money and never spends more than the limit you set.
  • Please have your payment ready. The rider has already paid for your food out of their own pocket.

5. Conduct, suspension and support

An account may be suspended or removed for any of the following:

  • Dishonesty about an order, a delivery or a payment.
  • Collecting or demanding more money than the app states.
  • Repeatedly cancelling accepted jobs without good reason.
  • Abusive, threatening or discriminatory behaviour towards anyone.
  • Tampering with orders, or delivering goods that are unsafe.
  • Letting someone else use your account.
  • Attempting to take payment outside the platform for a job arranged on it.

If something goes wrong, contact support in the app — Profile → Help Center — or message our Facebook page. Report problems while they are fresh and include the order number; it begins with a #.

6. Before your meeting

Please bring:

  • Riders: your driver's licence, a valid ID, and your phone with the app installed. Come ready to discuss how many points to start with and what working cash you can carry.
  • Stores: your business permit, a valid ID, and your menu with current prices. Photographs of your best-selling items help enormously.

Write down anything in this guide you disagree with or do not understand. The meeting is the right place to settle it.

One last word

The rules in this guide exist because real money moves between real people with no bank in the middle. A rider fronts their own cash; a store hands over food before being paid by a customer they never meet. That only works while everyone is straight with each other, and we would rather be clear about it now than apologetic about it later.
