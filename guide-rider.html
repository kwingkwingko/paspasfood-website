Rider Guide

How you get paid, what it costs you, and the rules.
Please read all of it before your onboarding meeting, and bring your questions.

Riding for PaspasFood is not quite like other delivery apps. You pay the store for the food out of your own pocket, and the customer repays you at the door. We take no commission from what you earn. This guide explains exactly how that works, what cash you need to start, and the one rule that protects you from losing money.

1. The basics

Where we operateSindangan and nearby barangays.
How customers payCash on delivery only. No GCash, no cards, no online payment.
What you deliverFood from partner stores, and Pasugo — errands where you buy something for a customer, up to ₱3,000.
VehiclesBicycle, motorcycle or car. You will also need a smartphone with mobile data.

2. Applying

  1. Download the app and register as a Rider.
  2. Upload a photo of your driver's licence and a selfie holding it.
  3. Complete the email verification step. Your application is not finished until you do — an unverified application reaches us with your documents missing.
  4. We review it and notify you in the app when you are approved.

3. PaspasPoints — what the platform costs you

We do not take a cut of your earnings. Instead you buy PaspasPoints, and one point is used each time you accept a job.

Price₱8.00 per point at present. This price can change — the current price is always shown in the app before you buy, and a change never affects points you have already paid for.
Cost per jobOne point, whatever the order is worth. A ₱1,500 order costs the same one point as a ₱80 one.
How to buyRequest the number you want in the app, then bring the money to the PaspasFood office. Requesting is not buying — points are credited only once we have actually received payment.
Maximum held40 points at a time. The limit caps what you commit to us in advance.
ExpiryPoints never expire.
RefundsNot refundable, not transferable, no cash value. They cannot be sold or given to another rider. Buy what you expect to use.

A rider with no points cannot accept work. Keep a few in reserve.

4. How the money moves

Read this twice. It is the part riders most often get wrong on the first day.

1. You accept the job. One PaspasPoint is held.
2. At the store, you pay for the food — from your own money. The amount is on the printed Store Copy and it is the food only.
3. Take the Customer Copy with the order. That is the customer's receipt, showing everything they will pay you. The store puts it in the bag — check it is there before you leave.
4. At the customer's door, you collect the full total — food, delivery fee, any weather surcharge, and any tip. It is the figure on the Customer Copy.
5. You keep the difference. The delivery fee, the surcharge and the tip are yours. PaspasFood takes no share of any of it.

The two pieces of paper you handle

Store CopyWhat you PAY. Food only. It says RIDER PAYS at the bottom. Stays with the store.
Customer CopyWhat you COLLECT. The full total — food, delivery fee, surcharge and tip. Travels in the bag and goes to the customer.

The gap between those two figures is your pay for the trip.

Never pay a store the customer's total

The slip the store prints for you says RIDER PAYS and shows the food subtotal only. The customer's total is a bigger number because it includes the delivery fee, the surcharge and the tip — which are your wages, not the store's. If a store asks you for the customer's total, show them this page and call us.

5. How much cash you need to start

Because you pay the store first and are repaid at the door, you need money in your pocket before you earn any. On current averages an order is around ₱200, and you may hold two jobs at once, so plan for roughly ₱400 of working cash per shift, plus your points. A large order needs more.

This is a real requirement, and we would rather you knew it now than found out at a counter.

Verify before you pay — the most important rule in this guide

Once you have paid the store, that money is at risk. If the customer cannot be reached, refuses the order, or the address does not exist, you are left holding the goods and you are out the money you paid. PaspasFood does not reimburse it and does not charge the customer for it.

Before you hand over any cash, check the order:

  • Is the order unusually large or expensive?
  • Is the address unfamiliar, vague or incomplete?
  • Did the customer answer when you called or messaged?

If anything feels wrong, call the customer before you pay. If you still cannot reach them, contact support rather than paying and hoping.

6. If a job is cancelled

What happenedYour point
The customer cancels before you collect the foodReturned
The store cancelsReturned
PaspasFood cancels, or the order cannot be fulfilledReturned
The customer refuses the deliveryReturned
You cancel a job you had already accepted, without a valid reasonUsed

Your point is used when you cancel because, while you held that job, nobody else could take it and the customer waited. If you genuinely cannot finish a job — a breakdown, an accident, an emergency — tell support. We are reasonable about real problems.

A returned point does not return money you already paid a store. That is what section 5 is about.

7. Rules for riders

  • Hold no more than two active jobs at a time. The app enforces this.
  • A photo is required as proof of delivery. You cannot complete a job without one.
  • Check the order against the slip before you pay the store. Once you have paid, a wrong or missing item is yours to resolve with them.
  • Do not open, consume, substitute or tamper with an order.
  • Do not ask a customer for more than the total shown in the app, for any reason.
  • Keep your phone reachable while you hold a job. Customers are told they can contact you.
  • Treat customers and store staff courteously, whatever the situation.
  • Obey traffic law. Wear a helmet. No delivery is worth an injury.
  • Your account is yours. Do not let anyone else ride on it.

8. Losing your account

An account may be suspended or removed for:

  • Dishonesty about an order, a delivery or a payment.
  • Collecting or demanding more money than the app states.
  • Repeatedly cancelling accepted jobs without good reason.
  • Abusive, threatening or discriminatory behaviour towards anyone.
  • Tampering with orders, or delivering goods that are unsafe.
  • Letting someone else use your account.
  • Taking payment outside the platform for a job arranged on it.

If something goes wrong, contact support in the app — Profile → Help Center — or message our Facebook page. Report problems while they are fresh and include the order number; it begins with a #.

9. Before your meeting

Please bring your driver's licence, a valid ID, and your phone with the app installed.

Come ready to discuss two things: how many points you want to start with, and how much working cash you can comfortably carry on a shift.

Write down anything here you disagree with or do not understand. The meeting is the right place to settle it.

One last word

These rules exist because real money moves between real people with no bank in the middle. You front your own cash; a store hands over food before being paid by a customer they never meet. That only works while everyone is straight with each other, and we would rather be clear about it now than apologetic about it later.
