Riding for PaspasFood

Everything you need to start earning, in the order you will meet it. If you only read one part, read how the money works — it is different from most delivery apps.

Step by step

Getting Started

1

Sign up as a rider

Install the app, create an account and choose Rider. You will need a mobile number, your vehicle type, and your driver's licence number.

2

Wait for approval

An admin checks your details before you can accept work. You will not see jobs until your account is approved — that is normal, not a fault.

3

Get PaspasPoints

You need at least one point to accept a job. Request points from the Points screen in the app; an admin credits them to your account.

PaspasPoints

One Point, One Job

This is how PaspasFood earns. There is no commission on anything you deliver.

🎟️

What a point costs

₱8 per point. You can hold up to 40 at a time, so the most you ever have tied up is ₱320.

✅

What it buys

Accepting one job — a food order, or a Pasugo errand. After that, every peso the customer pays for delivery is yours.

At zero points you cannot accept work. The job board will look empty or every job will refuse to open. That is not a bug — top up from the Points screen and they come back.

Important

How the Money Works

You handle the cash. PaspasFood never touches it.

1

You pay the store

At pickup you pay the shop for the food or goods, in cash, out of your own pocket. Keep enough float on you to cover a job before you accept it.

2

The customer pays you

At the door the customer hands you the full amount — the goods plus the delivery fee. In cash.

3

You keep the difference

You get your money back plus the delivery fee and any tip. No commission is taken, and there is nothing to withdraw later.

Why your Wallet shows ₱0.00

Because you were already paid, at the door, in cash. The Wallet is a record of what you earned, not money waiting for you. There is nothing to withdraw and nothing missing.

On the job

Doing a Delivery

A food order

  1. Accept from the job board (costs 1 point)
  2. Go to the store and pay for the order
  3. Mark it picked up
  4. Deliver, take payment, upload proof

A Pasugo errand

  1. Read the shopping list and the spending limit
  2. Buy what you can, within the limit
  3. Keep the receipt — the customer pays what you actually spent
  4. Deliver and collect goods + delivery fee

Never spend more than the Pasugo limit. The customer agreed to that amount and nothing above it. If the goods cost more, buy less or contact them through the app before paying.
