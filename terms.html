4. User Roles

PaspasFood supports three user roles, each with specific responsibilities:

  • Customers: May browse businesses, place orders, and request deliveries. Customers agree to pay all applicable fees and provide accurate delivery information.
  • Business Owners (Store Partners): May list their businesses, manage products and orders. Store Partners agree to maintain accurate product listings, pricing, and availability, and to the obligations in section 9. Registration requires admin approval.
  • Delivery Riders: May accept and fulfill delivery requests. Riders agree to deliver orders promptly and safely. Registration requires admin approval and valid identification.

5. Orders and Payments

  • All prices displayed are in Philippine Pesos (PHP) and may include applicable fees
  • Delivery fees, service fees, and surcharges (e.g., weather surcharge) may apply
  • Orders are subject to availability and acceptance by the business
  • Payment is collected upon delivery (Cash on Delivery) or via other payment methods as made available
  • Coupons and promotions are subject to their specific terms and expiration dates

Pasugo errands. A Pasugo is an errand in which a rider buys goods from a shop that is not on the platform, using their own money, up to a spending limit you set. You pay for the goods actually bought, plus the fees below. Every fee is shown in the app before you confirm, and all of them are paid to the rider.

  • Delivery fee — for the distance travelled.
  • Shopping fee — for the size and weight of the list. An allowance is included free, so a small errand does not attract one.
  • Pasugo fee — for the value of the goods the rider fronts. Where it applies it is the larger of a flat base fee or a percentage of the goods, never both added together.

Fees calculated from your spending limit are quoted as a maximum and recalculated on what the rider actually spent, so you are never charged more than you were shown. A fee resting on a flat minimum does not reduce when less is spent. A rider may not spend more than your limit; raising it is your decision alone, and it raises the fees calculated from it.

What may not be requested. A Pasugo may not be used to buy anything a Store Partner is forbidden to sell under section 9.11 — goods that are illegal to sell, alcoholic beverages, tobacco and vaping products, prescription medicines, or weapons. A rider may refuse or cancel such an errand, and repeated requests may lead to suspension under section 13.

6. How Payment Is Handled

PaspasFood operates one of two payment models. Which one applies to your order is shown in the app at the time you place it.

  • Platform-settled. The rider collects payment on delivery and remits it through PaspasFood, which then settles with the business. Amounts owed appear in the wallet in the app.
  • Rider-settled. The rider pays the business for the goods at pickup from their own funds and collects the full amount from the customer on delivery. PaspasFood does not hold, transmit, or take a share of that money at any point; our charge to the rider is for the use of the platform, not a commission on your order.

In both models PaspasFood is the platform connecting you to the business and the rider. We are not the seller of the goods and we are not a payment service.

7. PaspasPoints (Riders)

Where PaspasPoints are in use, a rider buys points from PaspasFood and one point is used each time they accept a job. Points are a prepaid right to use the platform. They are not money, not a deposit, and not a security.

  • The price per point is set by PaspasFood and is shown in the app before you buy. A change in price does not change the price you already paid.
  • Points are credited only once payment has actually been received and recorded. A request to buy is not a purchase.
  • Points do not expire.
  • Points are not refundable, not transferable, and have no cash value. They cannot be sold or given to another rider and cannot be converted back into money.
  • There is a maximum number of points a rider may hold at one time, shown in the app. This limit exists to cap what a rider has committed to the platform in advance.
  • A point is held when a job is accepted and used when the job is completed. Where a job ends without being completed, section 8 says who bears the point.
  • PaspasFood may correct a point balance to fix an error. Every correction is recorded with a reason and the person who made it, and is visible to the rider in the app.

8. Cancellations and Refunds

Orders may be cancelled before the business begins preparation. Once preparation has started, cancellation may not be possible. Refund eligibility is determined case by case; contact support for help with an order.

Where PaspasPoints are in use, the point a rider spent on the job is returned when the job is cancelled by the customer, by the business, by PaspasFood, or because it could not be fulfilled.

Where the rider gives a job back themselves, whether the point is returned depends on the reason they select in the app. It is returned where the job could not be completed through no fault of the rider — the business could not provide the order, the order was not ready after a long wait, the customer could not be reached, or the address was wrong or unreachable — and where stopping was the right thing to do, such as an accident or safety concern, a vehicle breakdown, severe weather, a personal emergency, or the customer asking for an item that may not be bought on a Pasugo under section 9.11. It is not returned where the rider simply chose not to continue, such as distance or traffic, or where no reason is selected from the list: the job was unavailable to every other rider while they held it.

Failed delivery after pickup. Under the rider-settled model the rider has already paid the business for the goods before setting out. If the customer then refuses the order, cannot be reached, or is not at the address given, the goods and the money the rider paid for them remain the rider’s. PaspasFood does not reimburse the rider and does not charge the customer, and the rider is free to keep or dispose of the goods. Report a failed delivery in the app so that it is recorded; repeated failures may lead to account suspension under section 13.

9. Store Partner Obligations

A Store Partner is an independent business selling its own goods through the platform; PaspasFood is not the seller. In addition to the obligations that apply to every user, a Store Partner agrees to the following. These are binding terms. The practical guidance that goes with them is published in the Store Guide.

9.1 Treatment of riders. Riders must be treated professionally and respectfully, whatever the value of the order or the distance of the delivery. A Store Partner must not subject a rider to verbal abuse, discrimination, harassment or threats; must give clear instructions for pickup and payment; must assist riders promptly on arrival and avoid unnecessary delay; and must not require a rider to perform unpaid tasks unrelated to the order they are collecting. A safe and reasonable pickup point must be provided wherever possible.

9.2 Preparation times and waiting. A Store Partner must give a realistic preparation time based on actual workload and complexity, and prepare the order within it wherever reasonably possible. An order must not be marked ready before it is prepared and properly packed. Where a significant delay occurs, the preparation time must be updated in the app so the customer and the rider are told; a rider must not be left waiting without an update. Unexpected delays happen, and prompt communication is what is expected.

9.3 Availability and changes to an order. Product availability must be kept accurate and unavailable items marked as sold out. A Store Partner must not substitute, remove or change any item without the customer’s prior approval, must never substitute a cheaper or different product without telling the customer, and must clearly state any additional charge arising from a change the customer has approved. Customers are entitled to receive the goods they agreed to buy.

9.4 Listings and photographs. Descriptions, prices, quantities and photographs must accurately represent the goods being sold, and photographs should be of the Store Partner’s own products. Editing tools, including AI tools, may be used to improve lighting, background or image quality; images that misrepresent the actual product — whether AI-generated or heavily edited — must not be used, and serving sizes, ingredients and appearance must not be exaggerated. Photographs belonging to another business must not be used without permission.

9.5 Food safety, quality and packaging. A Store Partner must comply with applicable food safety and sanitation requirements and keep the necessary permits current. Goods must be packaged so they survive the journey: containers clean and secure, drinks, soups and sauces sealed, hot and cold items separated where appropriate. An order must contain the correct items, quantities and approved customisations, and must be checked for completeness before it is handed to a rider. Goods known to be spoiled, expired, contaminated or otherwise unsafe must never be supplied.

9.6 Handover and payment. Where a rider pays the Store Partner at pickup, the Store Partner must verify the order before accepting payment, may collect only the confirmed amount shown for that order, and must not impose any additional charge on the rider. A receipt or other proof of purchase must be provided where required, and the Store Partner must confirm that the correct order has been handed over. Any discrepancy must be raised with PaspasFood support rather than settled by pressing the rider, who is not personally responsible for the Store Partner’s preparation errors or for matters outside their control.

9.7 Cancellations and disputes. A Store Partner should not accept orders it cannot reasonably fulfil, and must notify the customer and PaspasFood promptly where an accepted order cannot be completed. A Store Partner must not require a rider to pay personally for a cancelled order, a missing item or a mistake made by the store. Responsibility for a loss, refund or compensation is determined on the circumstances, the available evidence, applicable law and PaspasFood’s published cancellation and dispute policies. No party is held responsible without the situation being reviewed, and all parties are expected to cooperate in resolving a dispute fairly.

9.8 Store information. Store name, location, contact details, operating hours, temporary closures, prices, product availability and preparation times must be kept current. A Store Partner that cannot take further orders should pause ordering in the app rather than leave orders unanswered.

9.9 Pricing and transparency. Displayed prices must be accurate. Prices must not be increased after an order has been accepted without the customer’s approval; amounts not included in the confirmed order must not be charged to a rider; sizes, quantities and options must be clearly stated; and promotional offers and discounts must be truthful and clearly explained.

9.10 Privacy of customers and riders. Contact details, delivery addresses and other personal information received through the platform may be used only for purposes related to the order they came with. A Store Partner must not disclose them without authorisation or another lawful basis; must not contact a customer or rider for unrelated personal or promotional purposes without an appropriate lawful basis; must not publish screenshots of private conversations or personal information; and must comply with applicable data privacy law.

9.11 Prohibited products. A Store Partner must not list, offer or supply through PaspasFood any product that is illegal to sell under Philippine law, or any alcoholic beverage. This applies to the listing itself and to what is actually handed to a rider: alcohol must not be added to an order, substituted into one, or included as a free item, gift or promotion. The same prohibition covers anything else whose sale requires a licence we do not hold or an age check nobody can perform at a customer’s door — including tobacco and vaping products, prescription medicines, and weapons. PaspasFood is not a licensed seller of age-restricted goods, no age is verified at handover, and the rider delivering is not acting as a retailer. A listing of this kind may be removed without notice, and supplying such goods is grounds for suspension under section 13.
